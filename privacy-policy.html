Stashly — Privacy Policy

Developed by Richard Klinger · Last updated: September 3, 2026

Stashly is a personal finance tracking app. This policy explains what happens to your data.

No account, no server

Stashly does not require an account and does not have a backend server. There is nothing for Richard Klinger to collect, because nothing you enter — people, places, denomination counts, crypto and metal holdings, activity history, your PIN or pattern, and your theme/language preference — ever leaves your device. It's all stored locally in your browser/app storage.

Third-party price lookups

To show live prices, Stashly fetches data directly from two third-party services when you tap "Refresh":

  • A cryptocurrency price API, for crypto holdings
  • gold-api.com, for gold and silver spot prices

These requests include only the asset symbols being priced (e.g. "bitcoin", "XAU") — never your name, holdings amounts, or any other personal data. As with any web request, your device's IP address is visible to those services as part of normal internet traffic; that's outside our control and governed by their own privacy practices.

Backups

The Export Backup feature creates a file containing your data and saves it wherever you choose on your own device. Stashly has no access to that file once it leaves the app, and no copy of it is sent anywhere.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Stashly does not include advertising SDKs, analytics, or any third-party tracking code.

Children's privacy

Stashly is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, since it doesn't collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a new "Last updated" date.
